EBA Score Bot

Project Story

This desktop app, which I wrote in high school to farm points on the EBA platform, was one of the first serious outcomes of my passion for programming. It no longer works today from a technical standpoint, but I keep it in my portfolio because it reminds me of my motivation to learn and problem-solving mindset at that time.

I built this bot in 2020 during the pandemic, when EBA was very popular. For me, it was not just a tool, but also one of the main reasons I fell in love with programming. Back then, without AI tools, spending hours coding, debugging, and finally seeing something work felt uniquely rewarding.
